softreset Technician Console 1.2.0
==================================

1. Double-click SoftResetConsole.exe. Nothing to install.
2. If Windows says "unknown publisher": click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
3. Click "Yes" when Windows asks for admin rights (you can say No, it still works).
4. Home shows a check-up of the PC: green = good, amber/red = needs a look.

Try it safely first:  SoftResetConsole.exe --demo

The app saves its data in a "softreset-data" folder next to the exe.
To remove everything: About -> "Delete all saved data", then delete this folder.
